Find your name

a–z, 0–9, hyphens. Length sets the price: 7+ chars = 90 PIVX, 4 chars = 700.

2

Your ownership key not a wallet

Names are owned by an Ed25519 key — the same trick every PiNS app uses. The difference: here it's a random 32 bytes you download as a file. It can never spend your PIVX. It only proves you own your names.

3

Where payments go

Paste the shielded address (ps1…, 78 chars) the name should resolve to. Anyone who pays yourname.pivx sends here. This is not the ownership key — the keyfile above is.

4

Sign & pay one payment

✓ Signed & self-verified. The signature was created in this tab; your key never left the page.

Scan with MPW / PIVXie, or send manually from Core, Coinomi, an exchange withdrawal — any wallet that lets you set a memo. Memo too long for your wallet? See the fallback below.

Wallet can't send a memo? — two-payment fallback

A wallet without memo support can still get you the name in two payments: first send the 90 PIVX here (no memo), then paste the signed command below into the second payment of 6 PIVX (update fee). The registrar attaches well-formed commands to whichever payment arrives — your identity, your name either way.
